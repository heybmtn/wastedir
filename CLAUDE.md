# WasteList — project conventions

- Don't describe WasteList as being for "paid" businesses only, or contrast it with
  free/council-run services (HWRCs, council bin collections) anywhere in site copy, docs,
  or content (pages, blog posts, README, etc.). The directory only lists commercial
  businesses in practice, but the copy should just describe what the site is, not frame it
  against free alternatives. Previously removed from README.md and several page templates —
  don't reintroduce this framing in new content.
